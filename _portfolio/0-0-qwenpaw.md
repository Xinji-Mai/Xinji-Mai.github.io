<p class="i18n-en">QwenPaw is an open-source personal AI assistant. My contributions cover models trained for its harness, custom Agentic RL datasets, and training and evaluation loops.</p>
<p class="i18n-zh">QwenPaw 是开源个人 AI 助手项目。我的工作包括面向其 harness 的定制模型训练，以及 Agentic RL 定制数据集和训练、评测环节建设。</p>
