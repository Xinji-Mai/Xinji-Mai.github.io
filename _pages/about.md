<section class="about-section" aria-labelledby="intro-title">
  <h1 id="intro-title"><span class="i18n-en">About me</span><span class="i18n-zh">关于我</span></h1>
  <p><span class="i18n-en">I am Xinji Mai (Shinji Mai, 麦新纪), an Algorithm Engineer at <a href="https://tongyi.aliyun.com/">Tongyi Lab, Alibaba Group</a>. My research focuses on <strong>Agentic Reinforcement Learning, Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI), Self-Evolving Agents, and LLM Post-training</strong>.</span><span class="i18n-zh">我是 Xinji Mai（Shinji Mai，麦新纪），在<a href="https://tongyi.aliyun.com/">阿里巴巴集团通义实验室</a>担任算法工程师，主要研究<strong>智能体强化学习、递归自我改进（RSI）、自进化智能体和大模型后训练</strong>。</span></p>
  <p><span class="i18n-en">I lead the development of the <strong>CuES Family (CuES, CuES-plus, and CuES-Next)</strong>, a set of scalable, environment-grounded data synthesis pipelines. They support post-training for <strong>Qwen3.7, Qwen3.8, and Qwen4</strong> across Cowork, productivity, and other professional settings, as well as for specialized model families such as <strong>QwenPaw-Flash and Piccolo</strong>.</span><span class="i18n-zh">我主导开发<strong>CuES Family（CuES、CuES-plus 和 CuES-Next）</strong>，这是一系列基于可执行环境、可规模化的数据合成流程，支持 <strong>Qwen3.7、Qwen3.8 和 Qwen4</strong> 在 Cowork、生产力及其他专业场景中的后训练，也支持 <strong>QwenPaw-Flash 和 Piccolo</strong> 等专用模型系列的后训练。</span></p>
  <p><span class="i18n-en">My research aims to raise the capability ceiling of models and agents. I see data as a foundational source of model intelligence, so I focus on building training data in executable environments and using feedback to guide post-training.</span><span class="i18n-zh">我的研究目标是提升模型和智能体的能力上限。我认为数据是模型智力的第一性来源，因此我关注如何在可执行环境中构建训练数据，并用反馈驱动后训练。</span></p>
  <p><span class="i18n-en">My main research directions are:</span><span class="i18n-zh">我的主要研究方向包括：</span></p>
  <ul>
    <li><span class="i18n-en"><strong>Agentic Reinforcement Learning (Agentic RL).</strong> I study reinforcement learning algorithms for dialogue and agentic tasks, and explore how capabilities such as code execution emerge through RL (<a href="https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/0adea62f2f98c5035aded00d4b2064e6-Abstract-Conference.html">Agentic RL Scaling Law</a>).</span><span class="i18n-zh"><strong>智能体强化学习（Agentic RL）</strong>：研究面向对话和智能体任务的强化学习算法，探索代码执行等能力如何通过强化学习涌现（<a href="https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/0adea62f2f98c5035aded00d4b2064e6-Abstract-Conference.html">Agentic RL Scaling Law</a>）。</span></li>
    <li><span class="i18n-en"><strong>Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI).</strong> I design algorithms and infrastructure for continual learning and autonomous improvement, from self-directed task generation, exploration, and attribution in <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10395">AgentEvolver</a> to the co-evolution of decision models and training data in <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24651">Piccolo</a>.</span><span class="i18n-zh"><strong>递归自我改进（RSI）</strong>：设计支持智能体持续学习和自主改进的算法与基础设施，从 <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10395">AgentEvolver</a> 的自主任务生成、探索和归因，到 <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24651">Piccolo</a> 中决策模型与训练数据的共同演化。</span></li>
    <li><span class="i18n-en"><strong>LLM Post-training.</strong> I study feedback-driven post-training and fine-tuning methods to improve training stability and learning efficiency on long-horizon tasks.</span><span class="i18n-zh"><strong>LLM 后训练</strong>：研究反馈驱动的后训练与微调方法，重点提升模型在长程任务上的训练稳定性和学习效率。</span></li>
    <li><span class="i18n-en"><strong>Environment Scaling.</strong> I scale executable professional work environments and use verifiable feedback to build training data. This work includes <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17670">Cowork-Gym</a> and the <strong>CuES Family</strong>.</span><span class="i18n-zh"><strong>环境扩展</strong>：扩展可执行的专业工作环境，用可验证的反馈构建训练数据，代表工作包括 <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17670">Cowork-Gym</a> 和 <strong>CuES Family</strong>。</span></li>
  </ul>
  <p><span class="i18n-en">My long-term goal is to help realize artificial general intelligence (AGI). I hope advances in intelligence will help people and intelligent agents live more freely and on more equal terms, with the opportunity to pursue their own aspirations.</span><span class="i18n-zh">我的长期目标是推动通用人工智能（AGI）的实现。我希望智能的进步能让人和智能体更自由、更平等地生活，让每个人和每个智能体都有机会追求自己的理想。</span></p>
  <p><span class="i18n-en">I earned my master's degree in Computer Technology from <a href="https://www.fudan.edu.cn/">Fudan University</a> and my bachelor's degree in Microelectronics from <a href="https://www.sysu.edu.cn/">Sun Yat-sen University</a>.</span><span class="i18n-zh">我在<a href="https://www.fudan.edu.cn/">复旦大学</a>获得计算机方向硕士学位，在<a href="https://www.sysu.edu.cn/">中山大学</a>获得微电子方向学士学位。</span></p>
</section>
